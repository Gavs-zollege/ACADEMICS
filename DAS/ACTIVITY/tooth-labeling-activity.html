Tooth Location

The teeth in the upper jaw make up the Maxillary arch. The teeth in the lower jaw make up the Mandibular arch. The Maxillary arch includes teeth #1–16. The Mandibular arch includes #17–32.

Numbering begins in the upper right quadrant of the Maxillary arch and increases clockwise.
